About Mrichhakotik

Mrichhakotik is a Kolkata-based Bengali theatre troupe established in 1998. Rooted in classical dramatic traditions and contemporary social realities, the group is dedicated to creating meaningful, politically aware, and emotionally powerful performances.

The troupe has received financial assistance from the Ministry of Culture, Government of India, and the Sangeet Natak Akademi. Mrichhakotik believes theatre is not merely entertainment, but a tool for reflection, resistance, and reform.
