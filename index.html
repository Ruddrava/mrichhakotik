Welcome to Mrichhakotik Theatre Group

Reviving Tradition. Inspiring Change. Crafting Stories.

Mrichhakotik is a Kolkata-based theatre group dedicated to blending classical, folk, and contemporary forms into stories that provoke, entertain, and engage. Our productions reflect deep-rooted cultural traditions as well as pressing social themes.

What We Do

  • 🏛️ Produce original and adapted plays
  • 🎭 Conduct acting and direction workshops
  • 🌍 Organize an annual theatre festival
  • 🤝 Engage communities through performance outreach
