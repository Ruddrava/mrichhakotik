Kantokito Mohakal

Language: Bengali

Duration: 1 hr 30 mins


Synopsis

Kantokito Mohakal is a sharp political satire examining corruption, moral decay, and the cyclical nature of power. Through irony, dark humour, and symbolic imagery, the play exposes the grotesque machinery of authority and the human cost it inflicts.
