Workshops & Training

Our Commitment to Theatre Training

At Mrichhakotik, we believe in the power of rigorous training and collective learning. Our workshops are designed to explore the depths of Indian theatre traditions—ranging from classical Sanskrit texts to folk performances—while nurturing contemporary acting techniques, direction, and dramaturgy.

Past Workshops

We have hosted intensive sessions on:

  • Voice, body, and breath control
  • Improvisation and ensemble building
  • Textual analysis and classical theatre (Bhasa, Shudraka, Kalidasa)
  • Site-specific performance and street theatre
