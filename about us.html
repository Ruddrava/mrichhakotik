About Our Theatre Group

Mrichhakotik is a creative collective founded with the aim of preserving, promoting, and reimagining Indian theatre traditions. Drawing inspiration from the classical Sanskrit play Mrichchhakatika, we blend folk, classical, and contemporary elements to tell stories that matter.

We strive to create bold, thoughtful, and socially resonant productions—rooted in Indian culture yet universal in their appeal. Our members come from diverse artistic backgrounds and share a commitment to collaborative storytelling.

Whether it's through original plays, adaptations, or interactive workshops, Mrichhakotik believes in the transformative power of theatre to ignite change and spark introspection.
