Our Festival Performances

Bharatiya Ranga Natya Yatra is a prestigious theatre festival that celebrates the diverse voices of Indian theatre. Curated by the Sangeet Natak Akademi and its regional centres, the festival features performances by theatre groups from across the country, each bringing their own language, style, and cultural perspective. It is a platform for artistic exchange, experimentation, and expression, offering audiences a unique window into the contemporary stagecraft and classical roots of Indian performance traditions.
